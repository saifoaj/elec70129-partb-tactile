# Roles

**Each of us leads one role; everyone still contributes to the technical work, presents in the video, and reviews the final submission.** With six members, each role has exactly one lead.

| Role | Leads on | Owns these marks most directly | Deliverables |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Coordinator and stakeholder lead** | Meetings, minutes, internal deadlines, Canvas uploads, the TA email; the stakeholder story | Problem and stakeholder (10) | Charter, minutes, stakeholder one-pager, final upload and email |
| **Research and novelty lead** | Reading-list papers, positioning against them, the novelty argument | Novelty (10); creativity (5) | 5-line summary per key paper, "what we add" paragraph, on-screen citations |
| **Data lead** | Finding, downloading and subsetting the dataset; licence; leakage-free split; data loader | Feasibility (5), shared | Documented subset, split by object/trial/session, dataset section of the README |
| **Modelling lead** | Baseline and main model; the main notebook; checkpoints; the `TRAIN = False` switch | Feasibility (5), shared; creativity (5), shared | Trained model, weights, clean notebook that runs top to bottom |
| **Evaluation and demo lead** | Metrics, confusion matrix or predicted-versus-true, failure cases, the demo clip | Feasibility (5), shared | Results table against the reference point, one explained failure case, demo clip |
| **Video producer** | Storyboard, recording schedule, editing, captions, the 5:00 limit | Presentation (10) | Storyboard, final MP4 with captions, closing card |

## Everyone, whatever their role

- Does a share of the technical work and logs it.
- Presents 45 to 70 seconds of the video in their own voice.
- Reviews the final video, code zip and contributions form before the coordinator uploads.
- Completes the peer assessment by Wed 23 Dec.
