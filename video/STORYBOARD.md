## Production rules

- One idea per slide, text of 24 pt or larger, figures rather than bullet points, never a full-page table.
- One slide template, one colour scheme, planned hand-overs ("Now [name] will show how our model performed").
- Sound matters more than picture: a quiet room, a headset or a phone microphone close to the mouth.
- Every speaker is named on screen. No AI voices, avatars or text-to-speech.
- Captions burned in or as a corrected `.srt`.
- Watch it together before submitting: timing, sound levels, captions, spelling.

Raw footage and the final MP4 live in Teams/OneDrive, not in this repo.
