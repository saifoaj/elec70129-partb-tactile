## How we communicate

- **Quick messages:** the group chat. **Actions:** GitHub Issues, one per action, with an owner and a due date. **Files:** this repo for code and docs; Teams/OneDrive for video and large files.
- **Reply time (proposed):** within 24 hours on weekdays, 48 hours at weekends. If you will be unavailable for more than two days, say so in advance.

## Meetings

- **Weekly meeting (proposed):** _day, time, room or link_, 30 to 60 minutes.
- Minutes are written in `minutes/` within 24 hours by the coordinator (or whoever the coordinator asks), with every action as an issue.

## How we decide

- Discuss, then a majority vote. The coordinator breaks ties.
- Every decision is logged in one line in `admin/DECISIONS.md`.

## If someone misses a deadline

1. Tell the group **before** the deadline if you will miss it, and propose a new date.
2. If a deadline is missed without notice, the coordinator agrees a new date with that person within 24 hours and minutes it.
3. If it happens again, the coordinator reassigns the task and minutes it.
4. If a problem is not resolved within a week, the coordinator emails the TA with the minutes, ideally by Mon 7 Dec.

## Ground rules

- Part A stays individual: nobody shares Part A code, results or report text with the group.
- Everyone contributes to the technical work, presents in their own voice, and reviews the final submission.
- Everyone keeps a contribution log in `admin/contribution_logs/`.
- All AI-tool use is recorded in our logs and declared in the contributions form.
